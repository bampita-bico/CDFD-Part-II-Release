`aromatic_chirality_stability.png` source-mix panel. Paper 11 includes capture
and overload-buffering CSV/JSON outputs with eumelanin recorded as a mature
endpoint exemplar, not an origin requirement.

Run `../make_interactive_panels.py` to rebuild `interactive_index.html` and the per-paper `interactive_panel.html` files after regenerating CSV/PNG/JSON outputs.
