\begin{abstract}
This paper isolates autocatalytic closure from later claims about alphabets, homochirality, or protocells. A reaction network begins to remember only when its products help regenerate the conditions that make them. In CDFD terms, this is the first serious biological use of $M_s$: retained organization through feedback. The companion script performs a conservative closure scan and reports sub-ignition persistence enhancement rather than completed metabolic ignition.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Papers 1--5 supplied energy, transport, compartments, and selective interfaces. None of these alone guarantees \emph{self-reinforcing} chemistry. Autocatalysis and cross-catalysis supply positive feedback: products accelerate their own formation~\cite{Eigen1971,Kauffman1986}. In open systems, however, diffusion and dilution supply negative feedback. Closure is the balance point.

Gap from Paper 5: catalytic selection must escalate into network persistence.

\textbf{Status: Inferred}

\paragraph{Capstone channel mapping.}
Metabolic closure is not single-channel: losing input $\Phi$, electron throughput $\sigma_e$, proton coupling $\sigma_p$, or stabilization capacity against $S$ collapses the bundled $J$ in the weakest-link sense even when $\Gamma$ inequalities locally favor amplification.

\section{Model and Assumptions}

\subsection{Network mass balances}
For species $X_i$ with concentrations $c_i$,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial c_i}{\partial t}
= \sum_j R_{ij}(\bm{c}) - \nabla\cdot\bm{J}_i + S_i,\qquad
\bm{J}_i = -\frac{1}{C_i}\nabla c_i,
\end{equation}
with sources $S_i$. Here $C_i$ is a \emph{transport resistance} for species $i$ (not to be confused with product concentration symbols in legacy drafts).

\textbf{Status: Derived}

\subsection{Minimal autocatalytic core}
\begin{equation}
X + A \rightarrow 2X,\qquad R = k c_X c_A.
\end{equation}
Well-mixed loss to a compartment of scale $L$ scales as $D_{\mathrm{eff}}c_X/L^2$ with $D_{\mathrm{eff}}\sim 1/C$ in the resistance picture. Then
\begin{equation}
\frac{dc_X}{dt}
= k c_X c_A - \frac{1}{C L^2}c_X
= c_X\left(k c_A - \frac{1}{C L^2}\right).
\end{equation}

\textbf{Status: Derived}

\subsection{Ignition parameter}
Define
\begin{equation}
\Gamma \;=\; k c_A C L^2.
\end{equation}
If $\Gamma>1$, exponential growth of $c_X$ results in this linearized well-mixed limit; if $\Gamma<1$, trajectories decay toward zero without other sources. The boundary $\Gamma=1$ is a mean-field caricature; spatial patterning and nonlinear saturation modify bifurcations.

\textbf{Status: Derived}

\subsection{Vector closure}
A network is \emph{closed} if every required intermediate is produced internally faster than it leaks, $\forall i,\; \sum_j k_{ij}c_j > (C_i L_i^2)^{-1}$ in the same schematic sense.

\textbf{Status: Hypothesis}

\subsection{Energy coupling}
Rates may scale with coupled fluxes $R_i\propto |\bm{J}_e\cdot\bm{J}_p|$ when redox drive gates chemistry~\cite{Lane2010}. We do not specify constants.

\textbf{Status: Hypothesis}

\subsection{Linear stability (Jacobian)}
Let $\mathsf{J}_{\mathrm{jac}}$ be the Jacobian of the reaction vector field. Local stability of a steady state requires $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda<0$ for all eigenvalues $\lambda$ of $\mathsf{J}_{\mathrm{jac}}$. Autocatalysis can instead produce unstable modes, limit cycles, or blow-up depending on constraints and saturation.

\textbf{Status: Derived}

\section{Main Results}

\subsection{Conceptual}
Metabolic organization begins when positive feedback loops beat transport losses~\cite{MaynardSmith1995}. Compartments increase $C$ (resistance to escape), improving $\Gamma$ for fixed $k,c_A,L$.

\textbf{Status: Inferred}

\section{Numerical Verification}
\texttt{supplementary\_ool\_06.py} integrates a reduced two-parameter caricature. Reported final $\Phi$ values:
\begin{itemize}
\item $(\Phi_0=0.1,C=1.0)\rightarrow 0.073$
\item $(\Phi_0=0.5,C=1.0)\rightarrow 0.554$
\item $(\Phi_0=0.1,C=5.0)\rightarrow 0.102$
\item $(\Phi_0=0.5,C=5.0)\rightarrow 0.849$
\end{itemize}
Higher constraint $C$ raises the final state relative to comparable baselines, but \textbf{no run exhibits runaway ignition} in the companion script. Narratives about ``critical metabolism'' must therefore remain Hypothesis/Open until bifurcation scans identify supercritical parameters.

\section{Interpretation}
The mathematics clarifies what must be proven: closure is a competition between amplification and loss. The numerics currently demonstrate \emph{sub-ignition} persistence enhancement, not autonomous self-acceleration to a living steady state.

\section{Modern Network Boundary}
Autocatalysis is not enough by itself. A closure claim must distinguish three layers: self-amplification of a species, mutual regeneration of a reaction set, and persistence of an organized network through environmental cycling. The modern CDFD reading is therefore strict: closure becomes biological only when it couples to chemical memory, boundary retention, and later copying. This is why Paper 6 ends before homochirality or genetic inheritance are claimed.
