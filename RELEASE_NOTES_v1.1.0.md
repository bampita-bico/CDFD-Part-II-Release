## Summary

This release updates the Part II origins-of-life archive with **claim discipline**, a formal claim-status ledger, multi-origin convergence diagnostics, and clearer separation between bookkeeping notation and historical/empirical status.

Part II remains an **open preprint archive**. Supplementary scripts are toy diagnostics. They do **not** validate any historical origin-of-life pathway.

## What changed from v1.0.2

### Claim discipline

- Removed `MUJJABI_LIFE_LAWS_AND_TESTS.md` from the active release surface
- `Psi_s` packaging treated as **notation / reframe**, not OOL novelty
- `Lambda_life` (Life Number) treated as a **gauge**, not an ontology of life
- Paper-12 “theorem” language demoted to analogy / hypothesis where applicable

### New scientific accessory

- Multi-origin convergence diagnostic surface for comparing scenario families without claiming historical resolution

## Claim boundary

- Framework symbols (`Phi`, `C`, `S`, `M_s`, `Psi_s`, `Lambda_life`) are release-internal modelling terms
- They do not replace vents, Fe–S chemistry, RAF theory, wet–dry polymerization, Eigen thresholds, or LLPS compartment literature
- Outputs are consistency / toy diagnostics, not empirical abiogenesis evidence
